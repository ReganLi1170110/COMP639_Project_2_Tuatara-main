🏆 How It Works

Every time you contribute to predator control and conservation, you earn Guardian Points. Reach point thresholds to unlock beautiful digital badges – and when you unlock a badge, you can claim a free physical badge delivered to your New Zealand address.

✨ No points are ever deducted – even when you claim physical badges. Your points grow with your contribution, and badges are yours forever.

📦 Claiming Physical Badges

Every digital badge you unlock can be claimed as a free physical badge – mailed to any New Zealand address at no cost to you.

  • Go to your Profile Page → Badges Gallery
  • Click "Claim Physical" under any earned badge
  • Enter your NZ delivery address (postcode must be 4 digits)
  • Your request goes to our admin team for fulfillment
  • Each badge type can be claimed once – duplicates are prevented
📮 Free postage within New Zealand – Your badge will arrive as a real-world thank you for protecting Aotearoa's wildlife.
❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do points ever decrease?
No. Points are only added – never deducted. Even if you claim a physical badge, your points stay the same.

Can I claim the same badge twice?
No. Each badge type can be physically claimed once per user. Digital badges remain visible forever.

What if I move after claiming?
Please ensure your address is correct at claim time. Contact an admin if your badge hasn't arrived.

How long does delivery take?
Typically 2-3 weeks within New Zealand. You'll receive an email when your badge ships.

Do Group Coordinators earn points differently?
No – all users earn points the same way. Some actions (like creating trap lines) are only available to Coordinators, which naturally gives more earning opportunities.
